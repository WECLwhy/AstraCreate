普通问题请提供版本、macOS 完整版本、芯片、复现步骤和预期/实际结果。诊断 JSON 与截图为可选，提交前自行脱敏。不要上传密码、令牌、剪切队列、整个容器或旧原始日志。不要为了排障公开系统密码或关闭系统安全保护。

For ordinary problems, provide app/full macOS version, chip, steps and expected/actual results. Diagnostic JSON/screenshots are optional and must be reviewed/redacted. Never upload passwords, tokens, pending-move queues, entire containers or legacy raw logs. Do not disclose system passwords or disable system protections for troubleshooting.

维护者按实际精力处理，没有承诺响应或修复时限。 / Maintenance is best-effort, without a promised response or fix deadline.
